PhD student · Computer Science

Pingshi (Jacob) Yu 于洴石

I'm a final-year PhD student at Imperial College London, supervised by Alastair Donaldson and Nicolas Wu. I'm interested in the theory and practice of developing software systems, more specifically, either finding bugs when they do occur, or avoiding them in the first place. My research thus far took me through journeys in compiler testing, language semantics, complexity theory and automated verification.
