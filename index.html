PhD student · Computer Science

Pingshi (Jacob) Yu 于洴石

I'm a final-year PhD student at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Alastair Donaldson and Prof. Nicolas Wu. I'm interested in the theory and practice of developing software systems: either how to find bugs when they occur or how to avoid them in the first place. My research thus far has taken me on a journey through compiler testing, language semantics, property-based testing, complexity theory, and automated verification.
